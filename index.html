Privacy Policy

DYUKSA IMS · Last updated: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DYUKSA IMS ("the app") handles information. The app is an inventory and order management tool provided by LensVox for use by authorised employees of subscribing organisations.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email address and username, used to sign you in and identify you within your organisation.
  • Organisation and role data — the organisation you belong to and the permissions assigned to you, used to control what you can access.
  • Business records you enter — products, stock levels, warehouses, orders, invoices, customers and suppliers.
  • Technical data — device type and app version, used to diagnose errors.

How we use information

  • To authenticate you and keep your session secure.
  • To provide inventory, order and reporting features.
  • To send notifications relating to your work, such as low stock alerts.
  • To maintain security and investigate misuse.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for advertising. Data you enter is visible to other authorised users within your own organisation, according to their role. We disclose information to third parties only where required by law.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS. Access is restricted by authentication tokens and role-based permissions.

Retention and deletion

We retain data for as long as your organisation maintains an account. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, contact your organisation administrator or email us at the address below.

Children

The app is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
